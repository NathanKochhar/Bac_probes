KAN2M3 GENUS PROBE DESIGN - RUN SUMMARY
=======================================
Date: 2026-09-29
Database: SILVA 138.1 SSURef NR99 (510,508 sequences)
Probe design: 32-mers, GC 35-65%, no homopolymers >= 5, exact matching only (no BLAST)
Machine: AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D (8 cores / 16 threads), 31 GB RAM

The "probe" column in every output file is the reverse complement of the k-mer;
this is the sequence to order for in-situ use.


KEY METRIC: PRECISION
---------------------
precision = target hits / (target hits + exact off-target hits)

i.e. the fraction of all SILVA sequences carrying the probe that belong to the
target taxon. Raw off-target counts cannot be compared between a taxon of 11
sequences and one of 136,000; precision can. Precision 1.0 = zero exact
off-target matches in SILVA.


NAME MAPPING (CSV name -> SILVA 138.1 name)
-------------------------------------------
Genus:
  Clostridium -> Clostridium sensu stricto 1
    (SILVA's plain "Clostridium" has only 2 sequences; sensu stricto 1 is the
     true genus - C. perfringens, C. butyricum, etc.)
  Prevotella: SILVA splits off Prevotella_7 and Prevotella_9; these count as
     off-target.
  Lactobacillus: SILVA 138.1 uses the post-2020 narrow definition.



==============================================================================
GENUS  (KAN2M3/Genus/)
==============================================================================

Goal: ~10 highly specific probes per genus with POOLED coverage of ~50%.
Individual probes may have low coverage if they are very specific.

Method
  - genus_pipeline.py reuses the bac_probes library (same k-mer filters and
    exact off-target counting as `find --no-blast`). It reads SILVA once and
    scores all 34 genera in parallel on 14 threads (~12 minutes), instead of
    68 full passes over SILVA for 34 separate find runs.
  - Candidates: k-mers present in >= 2% of the genus's sequences.
  - Precision floors tried from strict to loose:
    1.0, 0.999, 0.995, 0.99, 0.98, 0.95, 0.90.
    The strictest floor whose 10-probe pool reaches 50% pooled coverage is used.
  - Greedy selection: each pick adds the most not-yet-covered target
    sequences. Probes sharing a 16-bp stretch are skipped.
  - Top-up: if coverage saturates before 10 probes, remaining slots are filled
    with extra non-overlapping probes at the same precision floor, for more
    signal per cell.

Summary
  - 32 of 34 genera reach the ~50% pooled-coverage goal.
  - 18 genera have 10 probes with zero exact off-targets (precision 1.0).
  - Every pool that reached the goal has precision >= 0.90; most are >= 0.99.

Genera that could not reach the goal
  - Blautia (9%) and Enterobacter (9%) stay far below 50% even at 0.90
    precision. Their 16S sequences can't be told apart from close relatives:
      Enterobacter probes also hit Erwinia, Klebsiella, Citrobacter.
      Blautia probes also hit [Ruminococcus] torques group and uncultured
      Lachnospiraceae.
    Reaching 50% would mean accepting probes that also bind those genera.
  - Escherichia-Shigella and Salmonella have only 4 probes each: no other
    non-overlapping probes reached 0.995 precision. The 4 still cover ~97%.

==============================================================================
CAVEATS
==============================================================================
  - No BLAST was run. "Zero off-targets" means no EXACT 32-bp match in SILVA.
    Probes that nearly match another taxon with 1-2 mismatches, especially
    near the probe ends, could still bind. To check the final lists:
      bac-probes validate <silva.fasta.gz> <probes.csv> --blast
  - Coverage and specificity are measured against SILVA 138.1 only. Taxa
    under-represented in SILVA may behave differently in real samples.
