The 'probe' column is the reverse complement of the k-mer; this is the
sequence to order for in-situ use.

THINGS TO CHECK BEFORE ORDERING
-------------------------------
  - Plant and animal matches: probes 5 and 7 exactly match 11 and 18
    Embryophyta/Metazoa sequences in SILVA (see top_offtargets in
    validation_results.tsv); probes 3, 8 and 15 match 2 each. These may be
    mislabelled entries or organelle-derived sequences, but for host tissue
    check probes 5 and 7 against the host's own rRNA.
  - Organelles: SILVA classifies chloroplasts (5,049 sequences) and some
    mitochondria (1,188) as Bacteria, so they count as TARGET here; binding to
    organelle rRNA cannot show up as an off-target.
  - Overlapping probes: probes are kept at distinct sites only if they share
    no 16-bp stretch, so some overlap by up to 15 bp (1 and 12 by 15 bp, 9 and
    10 by 14 bp; 8, 11 and 15 all sit on the EUB338 site as sequence variants).
    Variants that bind different organisms are fine, but probes that overlap
    on the same molecule would compete for it.
  - Coverage counts exact 32-bp matches only; probes also near-match many
    more sequences (BLAST target hits), so real hybridisation coverage is
    likely higher.
  - Large-subunit rRNA (23S/28S) is not in this database, so binding to it is
    not checked. Specificity is measured against SILVA 138.1 only.
