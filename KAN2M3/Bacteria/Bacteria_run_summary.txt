UNIVERSAL BACTERIA PROBE SET - RUN SUMMARY
==========================================
Run: bac-probes 0.1.0, 2026-10-02
Database: SILVA 138.1 SSURef NR99 (510,508 sequences) + SILVA BLAST database
Target: domain Bacteria (431,575 sequences). Off-targets: Archaea (20,389) and
Eukaryota (58,544).

Commands (exact; also in command.txt and validate.log):
  bac-probes design silva_db/SILVA_138.1_SSURef_NR99_tax_silva_full_align_trunc.fasta.gz Bacteria --level domain --min-conservation 0.05 --n-probes 15 --min-gap 10 --pool-target 0.97 --blast --blast-pool 40 --blast-max-target-seqs 500000 --threads 14 --no-candidates -o KAN2M3/Bacteria
  bac-probes validate silva_db/SILVA_138.1_SSURef_NR99_tax_silva_full_align_trunc.fasta.gz KAN2M3/Bacteria/validate_input.csv --blast --blast-max-target-seqs 500000 --threads 14 --breakdown phylum --min-seqs 500 --output KAN2M3/Bacteria/validation_results.tsv

The 'probe' column is the reverse complement of the k-mer; this is the
sequence to order for in-situ use.


HEADLINE
--------
  - 15 probes cover 95.3% of all bacterial 16S sequences in SILVA. The 97%
    target was not reached once probes were required to be spaced apart.
  - Probe spacing (--min-gap 10): on every bacterial sequence that carries two
    of these probes, their binding sites are at least 10 bp apart, so they do
    not compete for the same rRNA molecule. Probes that are variants of one
    site (e.g. 4 and 10, 9 and 13, 12 and 15) never occur on the same sequence
    and are allowed.
  - Specificity: selected at floor 0.999 on BLAST weighted specificity; every
    probe scores >= 0.9999, none hit the BLAST hit cap, and there are only 4
    exact off-target matches in total.
  - Probe 6 contains EUB338 (GCTGCCTCCCGTAGGAGT) exactly.


EFFECT OF --min-gap (vs the same run without spacing)
-----------------------------------------------------
  Without spacing, 15 probes reached 97.5%, but some overlapped by up to
  15 bp on the same molecules. Requiring 10 bp between probes removed several
  gap-filling probes for environmental phyla, which bound right next to the
  broad universal probes:

    Phylum              no spacing   --min-gap 10
    All bacteria           97.5%        95.3%
    Bacteroidota             97%          97%
    Planctomycetota          76%          21%
    Patescibacteria          55%          24%
    Chloroflexi              79%          70%
    Verrucomicrobiota        86%          79%
    Cyanobacteria            92%          88%

  Human-associated phyla are unaffected (Proteobacteria 99%, Firmicutes 100%,
  Bacteroidota 97%, Actinobacteriota 100%). The probes with plant/animal
  matches in the unspaced set (11 and 18 exact matches) are no longer picked.

  a separate design run targeting just these phyla.


CAVEATS
-------
  - Organelles: SILVA classifies chloroplasts (5,049 sequences) and some
    mitochondria (1,188) as Bacteria, so they count as TARGET here; binding to
    organelle rRNA cannot show up as an off-target.
  - Coverage counts exact 32-bp matches only; probes also near-match many
    more sequences (BLAST target hits), so real hybridisation coverage is
    likely higher.
  - Large-subunit rRNA (23S/28S) is not in this database, so binding to it is
    not checked. Specificity is measured against SILVA 138.1 only.
