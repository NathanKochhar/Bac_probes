WHY MOST SPECIES FALL SHORT
---------------------------
  These are limits of 16S rRNA at species level, not of the settings:
  - Escherichia coli: indistinguishable from Shigella by 16S, and very close
    to Salmonella; one probe passes, covering 3.3%.
  - Campylobacter jejuni: near-identical 16S to C. coli; none of the 7 BLASTed
    candidates reaches 0.90 (the earlier species run found the best at 0.896).
  - Enterobacter hormaechei: member of the E. cloacae complex, which shares
    nearly identical 16S; no candidate is specific even by exact matching.
  - Prevotella jejuni: only 5 SILVA sequences; near-matches in sister
    species (P. denticola) outweigh them.
  - Cutibacterium acnes: only 79 sequences carry the species name while
    1,585 Cutibacterium sequences have none, so few k-mers look
    acnes-specific; real coverage may be higher than SILVA can show.
  For these, genus- or family-level probes (../Genus, ../Family) or a
  non-16S marker gene (e.g. rpoB, gyrB) are the practical options.


METHOD
------
  bac-probes design (see the command above and every setting in command.txt):
  - Candidates: 32-mers in >= 2% of the taxon's sequences, GC 35%-65%, no homopolymers >= 5.
  - Exact off-target counts against all other SILVA sequences.
  - BLAST (>= 85.0% identity) on up to 120 candidates per taxon at distinct sites,
    half chosen for coverage and half for exact specificity. Selection uses
    blast_weighted_specificity: off-target near-matches weighted by where
    their mismatches fall (central mismatches ~ won't bind).
  - --exclude-unnamed-congeners: unnamed sequences in the target's SILVA genus
    ('uncultured bacterium', 'Genus sp.') are not counted as off-target when
    selecting; they may be the target species itself. Strains and
    subspecies always count as the species.
  - Selection: specificity floors 1,0.999,0.995,0.99,0.98,0.95,0.9 tried strict to loose;
    the strictest whose probe set reaches 50% pooled coverage is used.
    Up to 10 probes at distinct sites (no shared 16-mer); each pick adds the
    most not-yet-covered sequences, then spare slots are filled with further
    specific probes. Ties break on the k-mer sequence, so reruns match.

CAVEATS
-------
  - Only up to 120 candidates per taxon were BLASTed; others were not
    considered for selection.
  - No selected probe hit the BLAST max_target_seqs cap. The cap warnings in
    design.log refer to other BLASTed candidates (very universal k-mers)
    that were not selected.
  - Coverage and specificity are measured against SILVA 138.1 only; taxa that
    are under-represented or mislabelled in SILVA may behave differently.
  - Species labels in SILVA are incomplete; results depend on them more than
    at any other level.
